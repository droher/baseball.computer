# Historical supporting tables

These are frozen outputs from earlier manuscript revisions, primarily July 14 and
September 4, 2026. They are not refreshed by the PDF build. Read each table's
recorded generation, query, and caveats before using its numbers.

The [current evidence ledger](../EVIDENCE.md) identifies which historical results
the revised paper retains and supplies the current PBP candidate report bindings.
In particular:

- Gate-version-2 `passed` labels are historical and do not describe current
  gate-version-3 evidence.
- Historical `location_side` class values are angle modifiers, not global side.
- Ablation and smoke-backtest summaries do not imply that their full run artifacts
  are retained or that those experiments provide confirmation evidence.
- The former data-anchored selection offset is withdrawn; the derived ground-ball
  fraction is a conditional lower bound, not an identified correction.
